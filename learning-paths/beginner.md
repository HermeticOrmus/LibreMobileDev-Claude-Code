# Beginner — mobile mindset

## Mindset shifts

1. **Stores are gatekeepers.** Build for review from Day 1. ATT prompt, privacy nutrition labels, in-app purchase routing through the platform.
2. **Offline isn't optional.** Mobile networks are unreliable. Design for offline reads + queued writes + sync conflict resolution.
3. **Performance is felt.** 60fps is the floor. Janky scroll = bad app. Profile mode for measurement.
4. **Privacy is a feature.** Permissions are scary. Ask for minimum scope, at the moment of need.
5. **Cross-platform is a choice with consequences.** Native is faster + more native-feeling; cross-platform is cheaper + faster to ship.
